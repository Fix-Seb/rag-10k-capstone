TESLA, INC. -- Form 10-K (excerpt, public domain)

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Manufacturing and Supply Chain
We have experienced significant supply chain disruptions in recent years.
Semiconductor shortages have constrained our ability to produce vehicles at
the volume we would otherwise have targeted. We continue to depend on a
limited number of suppliers, including for battery cells and critical
electronic components. Disruption in supplies, including those arising from
geopolitical tension or natural disasters, could materially adversely affect
our business.

Battery and Raw Materials
The availability and price of key minerals -- including lithium, nickel and
cobalt -- has been volatile. Our long-term cost roadmap depends on continued
improvements in cell chemistry, sourcing, and recycling. Tariffs and export
controls on critical minerals could further increase costs.

Product Liability and Autopilot
Our advanced driver-assistance and Full Self-Driving features have been the
subject of regulatory investigations and product-liability litigation. While
we believe our systems improve safety on average, individual incidents may
result in significant claims and reputational harm.

Competition
Established automakers and new entrants -- particularly in China -- have
introduced competing electric vehicles, putting pressure on pricing and
margins.

Key-Personnel and Concentration Risk
Our success depends on the continued service of our Chief Executive Officer
and other key personnel. The loss of any such persons could materially
adversely affect our business.

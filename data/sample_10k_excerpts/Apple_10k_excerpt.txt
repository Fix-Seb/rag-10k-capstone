APPLE INC. -- Form 10-K (excerpt, public domain)

Item 1A. Risk Factors

The Company's business, reputation, results of operations and financial
condition could be materially adversely affected by a number of factors,
including the following:

Macroeconomic and Industry Risks
- The Company is subject to global and regional economic and political
  conditions, including inflation, recessions, currency fluctuations, and the
  effects of trade disputes.
- The Company faces substantial competition. The markets for the Company's
  products and services are highly competitive and the Company is confronted
  by aggressive competition in all areas of its business.

Supply Chain and Operational Risks
- The Company relies on a small number of outsourcing partners for
  manufacturing of many of its products, including key partners located in
  the Asia-Pacific region. A disruption in the operations of any of these
  partners, including those caused by semiconductor shortages, public health
  issues, geopolitical tension, or natural disasters, could materially
  adversely affect the Company.
- The Company's supply chain has experienced periodic constraints in
  semiconductor and other component supply that have, at times, affected
  product availability and gross margin.

Legal and Regulatory Compliance
- The Company is subject to laws and regulations worldwide, including
  consumer-protection, privacy, data-protection, antitrust, and tax laws,
  any change to which could materially adversely affect the Company.

Foreign Exchange Risk
- A significant portion of the Company's net sales is generated outside the
  United States. Weakness in foreign currencies relative to the U.S. dollar
  adversely affects the U.S. dollar value of the Company's foreign-currency
  net sales and earnings.

Capital Return Program
- The Company has an active share repurchase program and pays a quarterly
  dividend. Future buybacks and dividend payments depend on cash generation
  and capital allocation decisions by the Board.

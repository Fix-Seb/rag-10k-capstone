PFIZER INC. -- Form 10-K (excerpt, public domain)

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Drug Pricing and Reimbursement
Governments and private payers continue to apply pressure on drug pricing.
In the United States, provisions of recent legislation, including Medicare
price-negotiation rules, may materially reduce revenues from certain
products. In Europe and other markets, reference-pricing schemes and
tendering processes continue to limit pricing flexibility.

Patent Expirations and Generic Competition
A significant portion of our revenues comes from products that face loss of
patent exclusivity in the next several years. Generic and biosimilar
entrants typically reduce sales of an originator product by 70% or more in
the year following exclusivity loss.

Clinical Trial and Regulatory Risk
Drug development is inherently uncertain. Many compounds that enter clinical
trials fail to demonstrate efficacy or acceptable safety. Regulatory agencies
may impose additional requirements, delay approvals, or require label
restrictions that limit commercial uptake.

Supply Chain and Manufacturing
We rely on a complex global supply chain for active pharmaceutical
ingredients and finished products. Disruption from quality issues, natural
disasters, or geopolitical events could constrain product availability.

Foreign Exchange and International Operations
A substantial portion of our revenues is generated outside the United
States. Movements in foreign currencies relative to the U.S. dollar may
adversely affect our reported results.

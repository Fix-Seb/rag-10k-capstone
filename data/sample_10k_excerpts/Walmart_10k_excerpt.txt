WALMART INC. -- Form 10-K (excerpt, public domain)

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Competition from E-commerce and Online Retail
We face intense competition from large online retailers, including Amazon,
as well as from category-specific online retailers, marketplaces, and
direct-to-consumer brands. Our continued growth depends on our ability to
operate a seamless omnichannel offering combining physical stores and
e-commerce.

Macroeconomic Pressure on Consumer Demand
Many of our customers are sensitive to changes in food and fuel inflation,
employment conditions, and government assistance programs. Persistent
inflation can shift the mix of purchases from discretionary to staple
categories and pressure our gross margin.

Supply Chain and Inventory
We source merchandise from thousands of suppliers worldwide. Disruption due
to natural disasters, geopolitical tension, or transportation constraints
could limit product availability. Inventory management at our scale is
itself a source of risk: too much stock results in markdowns; too little
results in lost sales.

Wage Pressure and Labor Relations
We employ over two million associates globally. Increases in minimum wages,
health benefits, and ongoing unionization efforts may increase operating
expenses.

Cybersecurity
We collect and process significant amounts of customer data through our
stores, e-commerce platforms, and financial-services offerings.
Cybersecurity threats and successful breaches could harm our reputation and
result in regulatory penalties.

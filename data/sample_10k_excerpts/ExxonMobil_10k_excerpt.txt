EXXON MOBIL CORPORATION -- Form 10-K (excerpt, public domain)

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Climate Change and Energy Transition
Government policies aimed at addressing climate change -- including taxes on
carbon emissions, restrictions on greenhouse-gas emissions, and incentives
for low-carbon technologies -- could materially affect demand for our
products and our costs of operation. Stakeholder activism and litigation
related to climate may also affect our reputation and operating environment.

Commodity Price Volatility
Crude oil, natural gas and refined product prices are highly volatile and
influenced by geopolitical events, OPEC+ decisions, weather and macro
conditions. Sustained low prices reduce cash flow and may require asset
impairments.

Geopolitical Risk
We operate in countries with complex political environments. Sanctions
related to the Russia-Ukraine conflict have constrained operations in
certain jurisdictions. Future geopolitical events may impair our ability to
develop, produce, or transport hydrocarbons.

Operational and Safety
Our operations involve significant risks including spills, fires, explosions
and natural disasters. Despite extensive safety programs, incidents may
result in casualties, environmental damage, and significant remediation and
litigation costs.

Carbon-Capture and Low-Carbon Investments
We are investing in carbon capture and storage, hydrogen and lower-emission
fuels. The economics and timing of these investments depend heavily on
public policy and emerging markets that may take longer than expected to
materialize.

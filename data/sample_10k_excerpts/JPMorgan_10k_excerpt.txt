JPMORGAN CHASE & CO. -- Form 10-K (excerpt, public domain)

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Cybersecurity
The Firm relies heavily on its operational and information systems to conduct
business. Cybersecurity threats targeting the financial-services industry
continue to grow in scope, frequency and sophistication. A successful cyber
incident could disrupt operations, lead to disclosure of confidential client
information, and result in regulatory penalties.

Geopolitical and Macroeconomic Risk
The Firm operates globally and is exposed to geopolitical risk, including
ongoing tensions involving Russia, the conflict in Ukraine, and competition
between the United States and China. Sanctions, export controls, and counter-
measures may affect our cross-border activities.

Credit, Market and Liquidity Risk
The Firm is exposed to credit losses on loans, market losses on trading
positions, and liquidity risk if funding markets seize. We maintain stress
testing, capital and liquidity buffers in line with regulatory expectations.

Regulatory and Litigation Risk
Banking is one of the most heavily regulated industries. New rules -- on
capital, liquidity, consumer protection, climate disclosure, and AI use --
may materially affect our profitability. We are subject to numerous legal
and regulatory proceedings.

Foreign Exchange
Our results are reported in U.S. dollars; substantial revenues are earned in
other currencies. Significant currency movements may affect our consolidated
results.

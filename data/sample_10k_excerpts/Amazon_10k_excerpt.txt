AMAZON.COM, INC. -- Form 10-K (excerpt, public domain)

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Intense Competition
The online and physical retail businesses, as well as the cloud computing
business, are highly competitive. We face competition from physical-world
retailers, publishers, vendors, distributors, manufacturers, and producers
of our products, as well as from other online and multichannel retailers and
marketplaces. We also face competition in the cloud business from major
technology companies including Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud.

Macroeconomic Conditions and Consumer Spending
Inflation, rising interest rates, and reductions in real disposable income
have affected consumer purchasing patterns, including a shift to lower-
priced goods and a reduction in discretionary spending.

International Operations and Foreign Exchange
We have rapidly expanded our international operations. Our international
businesses are subject to risks not typically present in the United States,
including different consumer preferences, regulatory regimes, tax systems,
and foreign-exchange volatility.

Fulfillment, Logistics, and Capacity
Operating a large fulfillment network requires accurate demand forecasting.
Over-capacity raises fixed costs; under-capacity disappoints customers and
sellers. The COVID-era and post-pandemic adjustments have made forecasting
significantly harder.

Cybersecurity and Privacy
We collect a vast amount of personal information from customers, sellers,
developers, and employees. Security incidents, fraud or privacy violations
could result in regulatory penalties and harm to our reputation.
